What do the buttons mean?

  • Again You didn't know it. The card comes back today and the interval resets.
  • Good You knew it with some effort. Normal schedule: next review in ~1 day, then 6 days, then roughly 2.5× longer each time.
  • Easy You knew it instantly. Same as Good but the next interval is 30% longer, and future intervals grow faster.

💡 Most people just use Again and Good.
